## How to actually use this for MANGOS-style prep

- **Meta/Amazon**: heavy on Trees, Graphs (BFS/DFS), Design-adjacent problems (LRU Cache, Trie, Twitter feed style), and behavioral-integrated coding (expect "why did you choose this approach" follow-ups).
- **Google**: leans harder into Graphs, DP, and problems with multiple valid approaches where they probe your ability to discuss tradeoffs (time/space complexity pressure-testing).
- **Netflix**: fewer pure-LeetCode-style rounds; when DSA does appear it's usually practical (arrays/strings/hashing) with more weight on system design and code quality/readability.
- **Oracle/Salesforce**: tend toward more standard, less "trick" problems — solid coverage of Arrays, Strings, Trees, and OOP-adjacent design problems (Min Stack, LRU Cache) goes a long way.
- Across all of them: being able to **derive** brute force → optimized solution out loud matters more than having memorized the exact problem.

Good complementary resources if you want the same coverage with even more structure: **NeetCode 150** and **Blind 75** overlap heavily with this list — most of these problems appear in both.
